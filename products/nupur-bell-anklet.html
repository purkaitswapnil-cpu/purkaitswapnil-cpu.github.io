Nupur Bell Anklet

A contemporary payal with nine small maroon resin bells on a fine chain.

Colour: Maroon with gold-tone

Description

Nupur is an old word for the ankle bells of classical dance. This anklet borrows the bell shape and makes it quieter: nine small bell charms in translucent maroon resin with gold-tone caps and rims, along a fine chain.

The bells are resin, so they make little sound. It is a payal you can wear to work.

Wear it with a cotton saree and bare feet, or with cropped trousers and flats.

Materials & finish
  • Translucent tinted resin charms
  • Gold-tone finish caps, chain and clasp
  • Finish: High-gloss

Cast resin carries small natural variations in colour, marbling and inclusions, so no two pieces are exactly alike.

Size & dimensions
  • Length: 23 cm plus 5 cm extender
  • Bells: approx. 0.7 cm
Care
  • Put your jewellery on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, and take it off before bathing, swimming or exercise.
  • Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing. Avoid chemical cleaners and ultrasonic cleaning.
  • Keep out of prolonged direct sunlight and away from heat. Resin can soften with heat and its colour can change with long UV exposure.
  • Fasten the clasp before storing so the chain does not tangle, and keep it in its own pouch.
