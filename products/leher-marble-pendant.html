Leher Marble Pendant

Description

Leher means wave. This asymmetric pendant has the soft, rounded edges of a river stone. Deep maroon and ivory swirl through it, with threads of gold-tone veining.

It hangs off-centre from a small gold-tone bail on a very fine chain, which keeps the look modern and lets the shape stand out.

Wear on the longer chain over knitwear, or on the shorter chain with an ivory shirt.

Materials & finish
  • Marbled resin with gold-tone veining
  • Gold-tone finish bail and chain
  • Finish: Glossy, softly domed

Cast resin carries small natural variations in colour, marbling and inclusions, so no two pieces are exactly alike.

Size & dimensions
  • Pendant: approx. 3.3 cm × 2.5 cm
  • Chain: 45 cm or 50 cm
Care
  • Put your jewellery on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, and take it off before bathing, swimming or exercise.
  • Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing. Avoid chemical cleaners and ultrasonic cleaning.
  • Keep out of prolonged direct sunlight and away from heat. Resin can soften with heat and its colour can change with long UV exposure.
  • Fasten the clasp before storing so the chain does not tangle, and keep it in its own pouch.
