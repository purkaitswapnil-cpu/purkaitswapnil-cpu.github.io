Description

Makrana is the Rajasthan town whose white marble built the Taj Mahal. This wide bangle borrows its colouring: soft ivory with drifting grey veins and the occasional thread of gold-tone.

It has a generous width and a smooth, rounded profile. Wear it on its own.

Wear with sleeveless white cotton, or as a counterpoint to a heavy silk saree.

Materials & finish
  • Marbled resin with gold-tone veining
  • Finish: Glossy

Cast resin carries small natural variations in colour, marbling and inclusions, so no two pieces are exactly alike.

Care
  • Put your jewellery on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, and take it off before bathing, swimming or exercise.
  • Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing. Avoid chemical cleaners and ultrasonic cleaning.
  • Keep out of prolonged direct sunlight and away from heat. Resin can soften with heat and its colour can change with long UV exposure.
  • Store each piece separately in its pouch so the resin stays free of scratches.
