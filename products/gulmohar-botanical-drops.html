Description

Every May the gulmohar sets Indian streets alight. These drops keep that colour all year. A scarlet bloom with its streaked ivory flag petal floats in clear resin, with a little fern, a sprig of baby’s breath and a few flecks of gold leaf.

A slim gold-tone frame outlines each teardrop. They hang from fine French ear wires, so they move easily and stay light.

Wear with a white chikankari kurta, or with a black tee to let the scarlet do the talking.

Materials & finish
  • Clear resin with pressed botanical inclusions and gold-leaf flakes
  • Gold-tone finish findings
  • Finish: High-gloss, crystal-clear

Cast resin carries small natural variations in colour, marbling and inclusions, so no two pieces are exactly alike.

Size & dimensions
  • Drop: approx. 3.3 cm × 2.1 cm
  • Total length with ear wire: approx. 5.5 cm
Care
  • Put your jewellery on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, and take it off before bathing, swimming or exercise.
  • Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing. Avoid chemical cleaners and ultrasonic cleaning.
  • Keep out of prolonged direct sunlight and away from heat. Resin can soften with heat and its colour can change with long UV exposure.
  • Store each piece separately in its pouch so the resin stays free of scratches.
