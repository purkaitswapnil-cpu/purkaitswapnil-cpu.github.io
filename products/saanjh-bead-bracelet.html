Saanjh Bead Bracelet

Round resin beads in the colours of dusk, with gold-tone spacers.

Description

Saanjh is evening, when the sky passes through rose, amber and plum. This stretch bracelet strings round translucent beads in those three shades, with a small gold-tone rondelle between each.

It slips on without a clasp and sits well alone or stacked with a bangle.

Stack it with the Sharbat bangle, or wear two in different orders.

Materials & finish
  • Translucent tinted resin beads
  • Gold-tone finish spacers
  • Elastic cord
  • Finish: High-gloss

Cast resin carries small natural variations in colour, marbling and inclusions, so no two pieces are exactly alike.

Size & dimensions
  • Beads: approx. 0.9 cm
  • Fits wrists of approx. 15–17 cm
Care
  • Put your jewellery on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, and take it off before bathing, swimming or exercise.
  • Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing. Avoid chemical cleaners and ultrasonic cleaning.
  • Keep out of prolonged direct sunlight and away from heat. Resin can soften with heat and its colour can change with long UV exposure.
  • Roll the bracelet on and off rather than stretching it, to protect the cord.
