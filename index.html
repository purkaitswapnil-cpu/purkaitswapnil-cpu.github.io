The inspiration

Old motifs, poured into something new.

Winniee starts with things you grew up around: a carved window, a tree in flower, a threshold drawn in colour. We translate them into resin, a material that holds colour, light and even petals inside it. The result is jewellery that feels Indian at heart and light enough to wear with anything.

  • JharokhaThe arched windows of Rajasthan, as a pendant.
  • GulmoharMay’s scarlet canopy, pressed into clear resin.
  • RangoliThreshold colours, banded around a jhumka bell.
  • MakranaThe marble of the Taj, with a thread of gold.
