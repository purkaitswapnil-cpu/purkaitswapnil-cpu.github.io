Our story

A little art, a little India

Winniee makes contemporary resin jewellery for women who love Indian design but dress for the life they live now.

Why resin

We fell for resin because it does something metal and stone cannot. It can be perfectly clear or deeply coloured, glossy or marbled. It can even hold a flower, a fern or a fleck of gold leaf suspended inside it. It lets us put colour and light into jewellery while keeping it light to wear.

What we mean by resin

Jewellery resin is a liquid that is poured into a mould or a metal bezel and cures into a hard, glass-like solid. That is different from acrylic (a ready-made plastic sheet that is cut or moulded), glass (melted and shaped with heat) and polymer clay (an opaque clay that is shaped by hand and baked). Pouring is what lets us layer colour, create marbling and set botanicals deep inside a piece.

What guides every piece

  • 01

    Indian at heart

    Every piece begins with something Indian: a jharokha window, a jhumka, a payal, a tree in flower, a threshold rangoli. We keep the feeling and simplify the form.

  • 02

    Contemporary in execution

    Clean silhouettes, generous negative space and colour that looks good with denim as well as silk. Jewellery you can wear on a Tuesday.

  • 03

    Made to be worn often

    Resin is light, so statement earrings feel easy and a wide bangle never drags. We design for comfort first.

The first collection

Twenty pieces across earrings, necklaces, bracelets and bangles, and anklets. Each one is named for the idea it began with: Gulmohar, Jharokha, Makrana, Rangoli, Chameli, Nupur. The names are a small reminder of where the design came from.

Every pour behaves a little differently, so the marbling, the placement of the botanicals and the scatter of gold leaf vary from piece to piece.
