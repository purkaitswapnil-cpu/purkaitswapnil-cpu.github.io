Exchanges

Start an exchange

Tell us which piece, why, and what you’d like instead. You’ll send it on WhatsApp, where you can add photos.

It is in your order message and on your invoice.

How exchanges work

  1. Necklaces, bracelets, bangles and anklets can be exchanged, unworn and in their original packaging, within 7 days of delivery: for another size or another piece (you pay any difference).
  2. We don’t take pieces back for a refund. Earrings can only be exchanged if they arrive damaged or incorrect, for hygiene reasons.
  3. We reply on WhatsApp with how to send the piece back.
  4. When it reaches us and we have checked it, we send your new piece. Damaged or incorrect pieces are replaced, or refunded if we can’t replace them.

The full policy is on Shipping & Exchanges.
