FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything people usually ask before ordering. Can’t find yours? Write to us.
What is resin jewellery?

Our pieces are cast in resin: a liquid that is poured into a mould or metal frame and cures into a hard, glass-like finish. Pouring lets us layer colour, create marbling and set pressed flowers or gold leaf inside each piece. It is different from acrylic (a cut plastic sheet), glass and polymer clay.

Is resin jewellery heavy?

No. Resin is much lighter than metal or stone of the same size, which is why our statement earrings and wide bangles are comfortable to wear all day.

Will every piece look exactly like the photo?

Very close, but not identical. Marbling, the placement of pressed flowers and the scatter of gold leaf vary slightly from piece to piece, so yours is one of a kind.

How do I find my bangle size?

We make bangles in Indian sizes 2.4, 2.6 and 2.8 (inner diameters of about 5.7, 6.0 and 6.4 cm). Measure a bangle that fits you, or check the size guide on any bangle page.

Are anklets adjustable?

Yes. Every anklet is 23 cm with a 5 cm extender chain, so it sits just above the ankle bone for most people.

How should I care for resin jewellery?

Put it on after perfume and lotion, take it off before bathing or swimming, wipe it with a soft dry cloth and store each piece in its pouch away from direct sunlight and heat. Our care guide has the details.

How much is shipping and how long does delivery take?

Shipping is free across India on orders of ₹999 and above, otherwise ₹79. In-stock pieces dispatch in 2–4 working days, made-to-order pieces in 7–10 working days, and delivery takes 3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your PIN code.

Can I return a piece?

Necklaces, bracelets, bangles and anklets can be returned unworn within 7 days of delivery. For hygiene reasons earrings can only be returned if they arrive damaged or incorrect.

How do I pay?

Online payment is opening soon. Until then, write to us from the contact page and we will help you order directly.

Do you gift wrap?

Every piece comes in a Taarini pouch with a care card, ready to give. Add a note in your order message and we will include it.
