Jewellery gifts by budget

Pieces to give at every budget, and what to know before you order a gift.

How gift orders work

  • At checkout, tick Is it a gift? We leave prices out of the parcel and print your note on a card inside.
  • Every piece comes in a Winniee pouch with a care card, ready to give.
  • Shipping is free across India on orders of ₹999 and above, otherwise ₹79.
  • In-stock pieces dispatch in 2–4 working days and made-to-order pieces in 7–10 working days. Delivery takes 3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your PIN code, so order early for a date.
  • Earrings can only be returned if they arrive damaged or incorrect. Necklaces, bracelets, bangles and anklets can be returned unworn within 7 days of delivery. Full policy
