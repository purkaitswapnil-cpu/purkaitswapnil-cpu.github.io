Size guide

Finding your bangle size and necklace length

Everything you need to order the right size the first time: bangles, necklaces, bracelets and anklets.

Bangle sizes

Our bangles come in Indian sizes. To find yours, measure the inner diameter of a bangle that fits you comfortably, or bring your thumb and little finger together and measure across the widest part of your hand.

SizeInner diameter
2.4approx. 5.7 cm
2.6approx. 6.0 cm
2.8approx. 6.4 cm

Between two sizes? A bangle has to slip over your hand, so the larger one is usually the safer choice.

Bangles in these sizes: Sharbat Translucent Bangle, Makrana Marble Bangle.
