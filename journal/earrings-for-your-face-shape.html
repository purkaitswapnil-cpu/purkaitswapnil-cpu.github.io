Styling guide

Earrings for your face shape

Six face shapes, what each looks like, and the earring shapes that balance it. Classic styling guidance, not rules: wear what you love.

How to tell your face shape

Pull your hair back and look straight into a mirror, or at a photo taken from straight in front. Compare the width of your forehead, cheekbones and jaw, and how long your face is compared with how wide it is. Most faces sit between two shapes, so pick the closer one.

Or let your phone do it: our virtual try-on reads your face shape in your browser and shows the earrings on you. Your photo is never uploaded.

Oval face

Balanced: a little longer than it is wide, with a gently rounded jaw.

Look for: Almost anything works: drops, hoops, jhumkas and studs.

    Your balanced proportions carry a long drop easily, and the teardrop adds movement without changing the shape.

  • Rangoli Jhumka Earrings

    ₹1,299

    A jhumka’s bell sits beautifully on balanced proportions, and this one is light enough for every day.

  • Amber Sandhya Hoops

    ₹899

    Round hoops sit well on balanced proportions, and the chunky amber adds warmth close to the face.

Round face

Similar in length and width, with soft curves at the cheeks and jaw.

Look for: Long drops and linear shapes that add length. Go easy on: Large round hoops and button shapes.

  • Gulmohar Botanical Drops

    ₹1,149

    A long teardrop draws the eye downward, which lengthens a rounder face.

Square face

A broad, defined jaw, with forehead, cheekbones and jaw close in width.

Look for: Hoops, curved drops and jhumkas that soften angles. Go easy on: Boxy, angular shapes.

  • Amber Sandhya Hoops

    ₹899

    The full circle softens the corners of a strong jaw line.

  • Gulabi Marble Sculpt Earrings

    ₹1,049

    Rounded, organic edges soften an angular jaw.

  • Gulmohar Botanical Drops

    ₹1,149

    Its curved, tapering outline softens a strong jaw line.

Heart face

Wider at the forehead and cheekbones, tapering to a narrower chin.

Look for: Jhumkas, teardrops and shapes that are fuller at the bottom. Go easy on: Shapes that are wide at the top and narrow at the bottom.

  • Gulmohar Botanical Drops

    ₹1,149

    Narrow at the top and fuller at the base, a teardrop adds width near the chin to balance a wider forehead.

  • Rangoli Jhumka Earrings

    ₹1,299

    Jhumkas flare at the bottom, adding width near the chin to balance a wider forehead.

Oblong face

Noticeably longer than it is wide, with fairly straight sides.

Look for: Studs, hoops and short drops that add width. Go easy on: Very long, narrow drops.

  • Amber Sandhya Hoops

    ₹899

    Width at the sides is exactly what a longer face suits, and these hoops are as wide as they are long.

  • Panna Geometric Studs

    ₹449

    Studs add interest right at the ear without adding length, a classic choice for a longer face.

Diamond face

Widest at the cheekbones, with a narrower forehead and chin.

Look for: Jhumkas, teardrops and studs. Go easy on: Narrow, pointed drops.

  • Rangoli Jhumka Earrings

    ₹1,299

    Narrow at the top and wide at the bottom, a jhumka balances the angles of a diamond face.

  • Gulmohar Botanical Drops

    ₹1,149

    Fuller at the base, it fills the space beside a narrower chin and balances strong cheekbones.

  • Panna Geometric Studs

    ₹449

    Studs draw the eye to your cheekbones, a diamond face’s best feature.
